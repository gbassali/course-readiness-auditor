# COMP 4000 — Software Systems Engineering

## Course Description

This course introduces the design and delivery of maintainable backend software systems. Students will work with API design, relational data modeling, automated testing, and deployment practices.

## Late Submission Policy

Late assignments are accepted for up to three calendar days after the published deadline. A penalty of 10% of the assignment grade is applied for each calendar day late. Submissions more than three days late receive a grade of zero unless an extension was approved in advance.

## Academic Integrity

Submitted work must be completed by the student unless collaboration is explicitly permitted. External sources, libraries, and generated content must be acknowledged where required.

## Accessibility and Accommodations

Students who require academic accommodations should contact the appropriate university office as soon as possible. Requests will be handled according to university policy.
